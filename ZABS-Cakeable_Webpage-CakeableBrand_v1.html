Admissions criteria

Who's a good fit?

We generally look for applicants who meet the criteria below. Admission is always at the judgement of the intake team and may vary by track.

Skills

  • Can complete a task (for example, folding a towel or wiping a table)
  • Understands and follows verbal directions
  • Can communicate basic needs (bathroom, hunger, pain, or needing help in an emergency)

Attitude

  • Is able to be redirected when off-task or upset
  • Is willing to learn and wants to work
  • Has a support network of family or caregivers

Common questions

Good to know before you apply.

Do I have to choose a track before I apply?

No. The application asks you to pick your preferred track, but you can also tell us you're unsure. We'll talk through both options with you at the Meet & Greet.

When do I start getting paid?

It depends on the track. Cakeable interns are paid from day one. ZABS Place starts with a 3-month unpaid sampling phase to find the right fit, then pay begins and continues through job placement.

How long does the program last?

Cakeable's internship is 6 months for every intern. ZABS Place is variable — paced to each individual's progress and skill development, sometimes up to 2 years.

What kinds of jobs do graduates go into?

Cakeable graduates typically move into café, hospitality, and food-service roles. ZABS Place graduates go into a wider range — retail, e-commerce, electronics, fulfillment, and more — based on the skills they developed.

Is there a waitlist?

Yes, especially at Cakeable, where openings typically run 6–24 months. If we can't bring you in right away, we'll share volunteer opportunities, socials, and workshops to stay involved in the meantime.

I need help filling out the form. Who do I contact?

Email hello@wearecakeable.org and we'll be happy to help with reasonable accommodations.

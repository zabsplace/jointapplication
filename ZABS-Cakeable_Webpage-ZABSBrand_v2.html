Admissions criteria

Who we're looking for.

We're seeking applicants with these foundational skills. Admission is always at the discretion of our intake team & may vary by track.

Skills

  • Can complete a simple task with support
  • Understands & follows directions
  • Can communicate basic needs in an emergency
  • Willing to be taught & supported

Attitude

  • Eager to learn & work
  • Able to bounce back from feedback
  • Has a support network (family, guardian, advocate)
  • Committed to showing up & trying

Questions?

Answers to what you're thinking.

Do I have to choose a track before I apply?

Nope! Tell us your preference, but we can also help you figure it out after your Meet & Greet. We want to place you where you'll thrive.

Do I get paid right away?

You start with an unpaid trial phase (2–4 weeks) to make sure the fit is right. Then you move into paid training at $9/hour.

How long is the whole program?

Plan on 3–6 months from application to graduation. The exact timeline depends on your progress & when we find the right job placement for you.

What happens after I graduate?

We place you in a real community job. Our coaches stay involved for a while to help you settle in & build confidence in your new role.

Is there a waitlist?

Yes, especially for Cakeable. Openings can vary from 2–24 months out. If there's a wait, we keep you engaged through volunteer opportunities & workshops.

I need help with the application. What do I do?

Email hello@wearecakeable.org & let us know. We're happy to help & can provide accommodations if needed.
